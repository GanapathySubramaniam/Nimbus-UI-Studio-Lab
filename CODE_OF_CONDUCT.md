# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Nimbus UI Studio Lab respectful, harassment-free, and accessible for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, security, and consent.
- Accept maintainer decisions made through the documented governance process.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the private contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
